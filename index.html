Felicity Assistant

Felicity Assistant is a private, personal assistant app. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and there is nothing here to download or sign up for.

What it does with Google data

With the owner's permission, Felicity Assistant reads the owner's Gmail messages (read-only) and reads and updates the owner's Google Calendar, so the owner can ask about their own mail and schedule.
