Felicity Assistant: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Felicity Assistant is a private app used only by the one person who owns and runs it. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: read-only access to the owner's messages.
  • Google Calendar: read and write access to the owner's events.

How the data is used

Only to answer the owner's own requests, for example "what is on my calendar this week" or "check my email". Requests are handled by software running on the owner's own computer. Answers are delivered to the owner through a private channel on a chat service (Discord).

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and any notes the app keeps are stored on the owner's own computer. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the owner.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

This app has a single user, who is also its developer. Questions can be raised through this site's GitHub account.
